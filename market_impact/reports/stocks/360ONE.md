## Business

Revenue comes from recurring fees on wealth assets (ARR), alternate and traditional asset-management fees, and transactional, capital-markets and lending income. A borrowing book (interest cost Rs361cr in Q2) funds margin lending and balance-sheet investments. Promoters hold only 5.9% and FIIs about 56%, so the stock trades as a high-beta, flow-driven proxy for domestic financialisation. UBS took a 4.8% stake via a Rs2,112cr warrant exercise on 2026-10-21/22.
