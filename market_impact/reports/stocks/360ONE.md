## Business
360 ONE WAM (formerly IIFL Wealth) is India's largest listed wealth and asset manager for high-net-worth clients, with AUM of Rs8.99 lakh cr (+31% y/y at September 2026). Revenue comes from recurring fees on wealth assets (ARR), alternate and traditional asset-management fees, and transactional, capital-markets and lending income. A borrowing book (interest cost Rs361cr in Q2) funds margin lending and balance-sheet investments. Promoters hold only 5.9% and FIIs about 56%, so the stock trades as a high-beta, flow-driven proxy for domestic financialisation. UBS took a 4.8% stake via a Rs2,112cr warrant exercise on 2026-10-21/22.

2. **FPI flows (+3.5).** FIIs hold ~56% and the stock's beta to the Nifty is 1.57.
3. **Theme: financialisation (+4)** and **tax policy (P_CAPITAL_GAINS_TAX -4)**, mainly a Union Budget risk.
4. **Funding cost (repo -1.5, spreads -1.5).** Borrowings ~Rs15,500cr; the 2026-10-07 hike and wider spreads weigh on a 39x multiple.
5. **Company-specific: new CEO and UBS partnership.** Profit slipped despite AUM growth (PAT Rs300cr versus Rs315cr a year ago), so cost and interest growth matter.

## Transmission examples
- **Oil -> equities -> fees:** Middle East escalation -> Brent up -> risk-off, FPI selling, VIX up -> Nifty falls -> equity AUM marks down and transactional revenue drops -> PAT falls with 1.3x operating leverage -> multiple compresses further. If crude eases (as after the US midterms), the reverse.
- **Rates -> margin lending:** 2026-10-07 repo hike -> bank loans and NCD costs up -> 360ONE's borrowing cost rises while margin-lending rates reset slowly -> spread income slips -> a high P/E stock de-rates.
- **Policy -> flows:** Budget raises capital-gains tax -> HNI churn falls and money moves to debt -> wealth ARR growth slows -> AUM-linked revenue falls.
- **Partnership:** UBS stake -> NRI/offshore referrals and global product access -> AUM growth beyond the +31% run-rate (positive, uncertain timing).
