## Business
HAL is a 71.64% government-owned Maharatna that builds and overhauls India's military aircraft and helicopters: Su-30MKI (licence), Tejas LCA Mk1A, LCH Prachand, ALH Dhruv, Dornier, trainers, aero-engines and avionics. About 97% of revenue comes from the Ministry of Defence. FY26 sales were Rs 33,089 cr (+7%), OPM 30%, profit Rs 9,116 cr, helped by ~Rs 3,900 cr of other income from a large cash pile funded by customer advances. Order book Rs 2,54,538 cr (31 Mar 2026) is about 7.7x annual sales, so the stock trades on conversion speed and the next programmes rather than on near-term demand.

## What moves the stock (ranked)
1. **Domestic procurement flow (+5 per +10% orders):** the DAC cleared ~Rs 1.1 lakh cr of acquisitions on 2026-09-07 including 138 ALH (HAL filing); AoN is only the first step and conversion takes 6-24 months. Shares rose ~4% on the news.
2. **Execution and engine supply:** LCA Mk1A deliveries depend on GE F404 engines (113 contracted Nov 2025; three delivered in Aug 2026 when supply restarted, seven in hand, ten more expected before Nov 2026 per summaries). Management guides ~20 Mk1A in FY27 and a ~31% EBITDA margin; slippage is the main earnings risk.
3. **Competition for future programmes (-6 per major programme lost to private players):** Citi says private-sector concerns are overdone (2026-09-09); AMCA and helicopter work are the contested areas.
4. **Geopolitics (+3 India-Pakistan, +1 Middle East):** episodic bursts of defence-sector buying, fading in weeks.
5. **Valuation inputs:** repo +25 bp ≈ -1.2; US 10y +25 bp ≈ -1.0; FPI flows +1. The pack shows a +5.68 elasticity to Fed funds (t=+3.1) with market beta 1.6, which I treat as a high-beta, rate-cut-regime co-movement and not as a causal driver.
6. **FX and tariffs (small):** USD/INR -0.6 (imported engines, kits), US tariff friction -0.5.

## Transmission examples
- **India-Pakistan escalation (4th order):** border incident -> emergency procurement and Defence Ministry fast-tracks AoN-to-contract conversion (2nd) -> HAL receives faster orders for helicopters and munitions-carrying platforms (3rd) -> defence PSU basket re-rates while INR weakens and oil rises; the negative leg is higher oil and weaker INR hurting the broader market, so HAL's relative outperformance depends on defence-specific flows.
- **GE engine bottleneck (3rd order):** US supply-chain delays -> Mk1A deliveries slip to FY28 -> revenue recognition deferred and penalties/LDs risk -> FY27 guidance missed -> earnings downgrades at a 36x multiple. Reverse path: normalised GE supply -> 16-20 jets delivered -> margin target met.
- **Rates/FPI (3rd order):** US 10y near 4.9% -> FPIs trim EM positions (FII share 10.2% -> 9.4% in 2026) -> DIIs absorb (DII 10.4% -> 12.5%) -> price supported domestically, limiting rate sensitivity.
- **China/Indo-Pacific (4th order):** China-India border or Taiwan tension -> higher defence budgets -> larger capital outlay -> multi-year order visibility and exports to ASEAN (joint production talks 2026-10-16) -> HAL's valuation premium sustained.
