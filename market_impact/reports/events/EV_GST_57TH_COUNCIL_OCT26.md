# 57th GST Council (8 Oct 2026): no rate change; process reforms (refund automation, decriminalisation) (`EV_GST_57TH_COUNCIL_OCT26`)
*india_macro · status occurred · date 2026-10-08 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence medium*

Council avoided mid-year rate changes and instead approved structural reforms: automated refunds (90% ITC refund automation), no arrests/lower prosecution and general penalty changes, easier registration, litigation reduction. Finance Ministry sources (8 Oct): GST rate changes to take effect only once a year, from 1 April. Backdrop (model knowledge): GST 2.0 slab simplification effective 22 Sep 2025 boosted consumption; gross GST Rs 2.31 lakh crore in Oct 2026 (+22.9% y/y), partly import-driven (CBIC disputes 'apples and oranges' comparisons). Opposition states sought compensation. Possible future items: mobile phones 18% review, flat 5% for renewable EPC.

**Already moved:** No rate-driven market move; refund automation is a working-capital positive for exporters/MSME suppliers.
