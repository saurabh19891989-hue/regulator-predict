# China re-imposes/hardens rare-earth and critical-mineral export controls hitting India's autos and electronics (`EV_RARE_EARTH_CONTROLS_REIMPOSED`)
*metals_china · status risk_scenario · date 2026-11-10 · P(3m) 0.12 · P(12m) 0.25 · confidence low*

Risk that Beijing lets the suspension lapse (10 Nov legal date or 10 Jan truce end) or tightens licensing (gallium 27 Nov; yttrium disruptions per ChemAnalyst 22 Oct). India's EV and electronics supply chains depend on Chinese magnets (ACMA, 2 Sep: India's EV ambitions hit 'roadblock'; HT explainer: China controls supply 'with the pen', not an embargo). India is building domestic magnet capacity (Oct 2026) but remains exposed. Probability judgemental: Chinese incentives to keep flows (Diplomat/China-Global South Project 23-24 Sep) lower the odds; EU supplies continued (Traders Union 15 Oct).

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `NEW_RARE_EARTH_SUPPLY` Critical-mineral supply tightness (China export controls on rare earths, magnets, graphite, Ga/Ge): 1.5 SSU (range 0.8…2.5) +1 = materially tighter magnet availability for India
- `C_US_CHINA_DECOUPLING` US–China trade decoupling: 0.8 SSU (range 0.4…1.2) escalation
- `F_SEMICONDUCTOR_SUPPLY` Chip/electronics component supply tightness (higher = tighter): 0.3 SSU (range 0.1…0.6) gallium/germanium spill-over

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_RARE_EARTH_CONTROLS_REIMPOSED → NEW_RARE_EARTH_SUPPLY → F_AUTO_DEMAND_IN → TVSMOTOR — Magnet shortages curb EV/2W and ICE component output (motors, sensors).
- order 3: EV_RARE_EARTH_CONTROLS_REIMPOSED → NEW_RARE_EARTH_SUPPLY → P_EV_POLICY → TMPV — EV roll-outs slip; policy support is discussed to build domestic supply.
- order 3: EV_RARE_EARTH_CONTROLS_REIMPOSED → C_US_CHINA_DECOUPLING → T_CHINA_PLUS_ONE → DIXON — Decoupling accelerates China+1 and domestic magnet/PLI plays.
- order 4: EV_RARE_EARTH_CONTROLS_REIMPOSED → NEW_RARE_EARTH_SUPPLY → F_AUTO_DEMAND_IN → F_GDP_IN — Output losses in autos (a large manufacturing GDP share) shave growth.
