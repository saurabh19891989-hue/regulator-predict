# Domestic LPG under-recoveries >Rs59,000 cr (to July); government compensation boosts IOC Q2 (`EV_LPG_UNDERRECOVERY`)
*sector_policy · status occurred · date 2026-08-10 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence low*

OMC domestic-LPG under-recoveries crossed Rs59,000 crore as of July (govt data, 10 Aug); per-cylinder loss fell to Rs188 in Aug (NIE) but accumulated burden persisted. Commercial 19kg cylinder +Rs62.50 on 1 Oct, domestic rate unchanged. LPG supply cost reported >Rs1,600/cylinder (2 Nov). Indian Oil Q2 profit nearly tripled on LPG compensation (Reuters 30 Oct). US share of LPG imports >50% (Sep) then UAE overtook US as top supplier (NIE 30 Sep). Domestic refill gap rule 25 days.

**Already moved:** IOC Q2 PAT swung to Rs21,630 cr; compensation size not verified.
