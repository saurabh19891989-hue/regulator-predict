# US waives Russia-oil secondary sanctions/100% tariff threat on buyers incl. India (17 Oct) (`EV_RUSSIA_WAIVER_OCT17`)
*geopolitics_trade · status occurred · date 2026-10-17 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence medium*

Trump waived the Graham-Act sanctions law mechanism two days before the 100% tariff on Russian-oil buyers (incl. India) was due. India's Russian crude imports stabilised at ~1.8-1.9 mb/d (Swarajya/Telegraph, 17 Oct); Russia retained top-supplier spot (New Indian Express 30 Sep) while UAE overtook US in LPG. India cut US and Russian crude imports in Sept, pivoting to UAE (The Hindu 30 Oct). Record Russian imports in July-Aug (ET, TOI 10-11 Aug). Nayara remains under sanctions pressure (SAP service order litigation, 9 Oct).

**Already moved:** Not separable in market data; Brent unchanged since 1 Jun. Removes tail of 100% tariff on India (other agent covers US tariff).
