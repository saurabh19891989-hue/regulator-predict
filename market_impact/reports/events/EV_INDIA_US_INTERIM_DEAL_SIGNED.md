# India-US interim trade agreement formally signed/announced (`EV_INDIA_US_INTERIM_DEAL_SIGNED`)
*geopolitics_trade · status anticipated · date 2026-12-31 · P(3m) 0.25 · P(12m) 0.55 · confidence low*

Talks have repeatedly 'stalled' (Reuters 6 Oct) yet US officials say '98-99% done' (16 Oct), Trump says 'pretty close, different from previous pacts' (21 Oct), and the White House already lists an India deal among 'promises kept' (3 Nov) without a signed text. A 'forward MFN' clause is under discussion. Hurdles: agriculture/dairy, Russian oil, US wanting competitor-country tariffs first (The Hindu 22 Sep). A narrow interim deal is the modal outcome. Midterm result may reduce US urgency or raise it. Current effective US tariff level on India is not verified from a primary source (legal basis shifted after a SCOTUS ruling; Greer says policy 'hasn't changed').

**Review override applied:** The interim deal is still stuck (BusinessLine 25 Oct). Reports after the midterms say India's urgency fades (IANS 5 Nov). The White House lists it as 'fulfilled' without a text. The current incremental US tariff on most Indian goods is ~10% (secondary sources), so a -10pp shock would take it to zero.

**Already moved:** Nifty50 +7.4% in 1m partly reflects deal hopes.
