# OMC ethanol tender ESY 2026-27: no cane juice/syrup/B-heavy ethanol; maize price incentive cut; grain-based ethanol takes over (`EV_ETHANOL_ESY27_TENDER`)
*agri_rural · status occurred · date 2026-10-17 · P(3m) 1.0 · P(12m) 1.0 · confidence high*

Oil marketing companies invited bids for 1,100 crore litres of ethanol for Nov 2026-Oct 2027 while barring sugarcane juice, sugar syrup and B-heavy molasses (CNBC-TV18 17 Oct; Hindu/ToI 18 Oct), i.e. zero sugar diversion; last year about 289 crore litres were initially allocated to cane-based feedstocks. Maize-ethanol incentive cut from Rs 5.79 to Rs 2.79/l, taking maize ethanol to Rs 68.86/l; broken-rice/damaged-food-grain ethanol priced at Rs 64/l; a feedstock-switch clause pays the lower price. Industry expects DFG/broken rice ethanol to rise from ~80 to >700 crore litres, raising competition with food/feed and maize. Grain ethanol is ~76% of supply (AIDA, July). Grain distillers pushing for price revision (FE, 2 Nov). Government argues (Aug) that only ~9% of sugar was diverted in 2025-26 and about 75% of ethanol is grain-based.

**Already moved:** F_SUGAR -11.3% over 1m to 5 Nov after the tender and imports; no distiller equity move quantified.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `P_SUGAR_ETHANOL` Sugar export / ethanol blending policy: -1.0 SSU (range -1.5…-0.5) Policy shift against sugar-based ethanol (negative for mills' distillery profits; positive for sugar availability).
- `F_SUGAR` Sugar price: -0.5 SSU (range -1.0…0.0) More sugar available for the domestic market, lower domestic sugar prices.

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_ETHANOL_ESY27_TENDER → P_SUGAR_ETHANOL → F_SUGAR — Zero diversion adds ~30-35 lakh t sugar to supply (289 crore litres cane-based), cooling prices.
- order 3: EV_ETHANOL_ESY27_TENDER → F_SUGAR → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN — Marginally lower food CPI.
- order 3: EV_ETHANOL_ESY27_TENDER → F_RICE → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN — Heavy broken-rice/FCI-rice demand for ethanol tightens rice/feed markets (poultry feed) and could prompt export curbs.
- order 4: EV_ETHANOL_ESY27_TENDER → P_SUGAR_ETHANOL → F_REFINING_MARGIN — Lower-cost ethanol supply modestly helps OMC blending economics (cheaper feedstock).
