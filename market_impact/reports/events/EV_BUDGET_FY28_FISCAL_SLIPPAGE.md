# Budget FY2027-28: FY27 deficit overshoot confirmed and consolidation path slowed (`EV_BUDGET_FY28_FISCAL_SLIPPAGE`)
*india_macro · status scheduled · date 2027-02-01 · P(3m) 0.5 · P(12m) 0.5 · confidence medium*

H1 FY27 fiscal deficit Rs 7.14 lakh crore = 42.1% of full-year BE (CGA, 30 Oct; 41.9% at end-Aug); 'five-year high' for H1. SBI Research / ET (2 Nov): FY27 deficit may exceed target by up to Rs 1.2 lakh crore (~0.3% of GDP) on subsidy (fertiliser/LPG/food, oil shock). The Budget may show revised FY27 deficit above BE and a FY28 target above the previously signalled glide path (event defined as FY28 target >=4.0% of GDP or RE overshoot >=0.25% GDP). LTCG relief would cost ~Rs 50,000 cr in FY28 (SBI Research). Probability 0.50 (government may offset via stronger tax/GST receipts: GST Oct +22.9%).
