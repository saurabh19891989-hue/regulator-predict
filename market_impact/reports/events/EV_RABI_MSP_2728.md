# Cabinet raises rabi MSPs for 2027-28: wheat only +Rs 25 to Rs 2,610/q, oilseeds/pulses by more (`EV_RABI_MSP_2728`)
*agri_rural · status occurred · date 2026-09-30 · P(3m) 1.0 · P(12m) 1.0 · confidence high*

CCEA (30 Sep 2026): wheat MSP +Rs 25 to Rs 2,610/q (about +1.0%); barley +Rs 136 to Rs 2,286/q; gram +Rs 83; lentil +Rs 390; rapeseed-mustard +Rs 413; safflower +Rs 675 (largest); estimated payout Rs 90,962 crore. The muted wheat hike reflects ample FCI stocks and prior low wheat prices; the larger oilseed/pulses increases aim to cut edible-oil and pulses imports. Same day: PM-DHARA transmission and BESS scheme (Rs 1,86,405 crore, 135 GW RE evacuation, 50 GWh BESS; not rural) and Green Energy Corridor Phase III approved.

**Already moved:** No traded reaction attributable.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN` India food inflation: 0.1 SSU (range 0.0…0.2) Small cost-push through procurement prices (wheat +1%).
- `F_RURAL_DEMAND` Rural consumption growth: 0.05 SSU (range 0.0…0.15) Modest farm-income support; muted vs inflation of 5.5%.

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_RABI_MSP_2728 → F_RURAL_DEMAND → F_AUTO_DEMAND_IN — Real MSP gain small, so little rural-demand lift for 2W/tractors.
- order 3: EV_RABI_MSP_2728 → F_EDIBLE_OIL → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN — Higher mustard/safflower MSP raises domestic oilseed floor prices (+413 on mustard) and edible oil costs.
- order 4: EV_RABI_MSP_2728 → F_RURAL_DEMAND → F_URBAN_DEMAND → F_GDP_IN — Not meaningful for GDP.
