# DGTR opens wave of anti-dumping probes on Chinese chemicals, APIs and steel (`EV_INDIA_ANTIDUMPING_WAVE_SEP_OCT26`)
*metals_china · status occurred · date 2026-09-22 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence medium*

Between late Sep and mid Oct 2026 DGTR initiated probes on glycine (22 Sep), seven Chinese products incl. key drug ingredients such as Pen-G and salts (29 Sep, 10 Oct), five further products (5-8 Oct), textile chemicals (6 Oct), caprolactam from China/Russia/Thailand/US (11 Oct); a hot-rolled flat steel probe on China/Japan/Russia and CRGO/amorphous metal probes opened 26-27 Jun 2026. Duties extended on phthalic anhydride (13 Aug, 5 years), melamine proposed (29 Sep), titanium dioxide duty promised by the Centre (23 Sep). ET (5 Aug) notes final duties 'crawl' because the finance ministry often does not notify DGTR recommendations - probes are not duties. Context: India-China trade deficit >$100bn in 2026 (The Hindu 14 Oct), India-China thaw (Xi Delhi visit, Press Note easing).

**Already moved:** Probes initiated; no duty yet imposed on most. Impact so far is sentiment (Trade Brains 30 Sep 'stocks to benefit').
