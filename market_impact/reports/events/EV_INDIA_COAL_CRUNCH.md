# India coal crunch: critical plant stocks, e-auction premiums ~94%, captive plants ordered to maximise output; CIL October output +19% (`EV_INDIA_COAL_CRUNCH`)
*energy · status occurred · date 2026-09-07 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence medium*

Early Sept 61 thermal plants at critical stocks (PSU Watch 8 Sep); by 21 Sep 74 plants had <=7 days (CEA/Business Standard); over half critical on 21 Oct (TOI). Government invoked emergency powers (captive plants to run full capacity, 26-28 Sep; Sec 11 for imported-coal plants such as Mundra extended to 31 Dec). A mandatory imported-coal blending plan was weighed (Reuters 25 Sep) then shelved (23 Oct). E-auction premiums reached ~94% (domain-b 12 Oct). Coal India October output +19.3% to 67.2 Mt, offtake +~19% (BS/CNBC 1-2 Nov); CIL allocated 118 lakh t in October e-auctions; CIL Q2 PAT -31.6% y/y as costs rose and shares fell ~4% on results, then +5% on October volumes. Rail loading 465 rakes/day (The Hindu 9 Oct).

**Already moved:** Seaborne thermal coal flat (96, 0% since 1 Jun). COALINDIA +~5% on 2 Nov.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `F_COAL_INDIA` Indian domestic coal price & availability cost: 1.5 SSU (range 0.8…2.5) e-auction premium to notified price up to ~94%; notified price unchanged
- `F_POWER_PRICE_IN` Indian power exchange (IEX) prices: 1.0 SSU (range 0.5…2.0) shortage feeds spot

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_INDIA_COAL_CRUNCH → F_COAL_INDIA → F_POWER_PRICE_IN — scarcity pushes merchant power prices
- order 3: EV_INDIA_COAL_CRUNCH → F_COAL_INDIA → F_POWER_PRICE_IN → F_CPI_IN — power cost pass-through to discoms/industry adds modestly to CPI
- order 4: EV_INDIA_COAL_CRUNCH → F_COAL_INDIA → F_STEEL_INDIA → F_GDP_IN — energy-intensive industry cost and rationing risk
