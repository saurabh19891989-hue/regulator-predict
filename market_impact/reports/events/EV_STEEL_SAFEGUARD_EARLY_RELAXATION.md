# India relaxes steel import curbs early (safeguard cut/exemptions) after inflation or FTA pressure (`EV_STEEL_SAFEGUARD_EARLY_RELAXATION`)
*metals_china · status risk_scenario · date 2027-02-01 · P(3m) 0.07 · P(12m) 0.15 · confidence low*

Downstream users (auto, pipes, capital goods) lobby against high steel prices; Indian Express (2 Nov) and LinkedIn (7 Oct) pieces question protection; trade deals (UK: 1.1 Mt duty-free steel; EU FTA 1.64 Mt quota under discussion) create carve-outs. Union Budget (1 Feb 2027) is a natural trigger. Honest odds low because ministry signals no appetite for change and domestic mills remain below capacity-utilisation targets.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `P_STEEL_DUTY_IN` Indian safeguard/anti-dumping duty on steel imports: -0.8 SSU (range -1.5…-0.3) relaxation of ~5-10 pp
- `F_STEEL_INDIA` Indian domestic steel price: -0.5 SSU (range -1.0…-0.2) via edge

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_STEEL_SAFEGUARD_EARLY_RELAXATION → P_STEEL_DUTY_IN → F_STEEL_INDIA → JSWSTEEL — Lower import parity cuts realisations.
- order 3: EV_STEEL_SAFEGUARD_EARLY_RELAXATION → F_STEEL_INDIA → F_CPI_IN — Eases inflation
- order 4: EV_STEEL_SAFEGUARD_EARLY_RELAXATION → F_STEEL_INDIA → F_GOVT_CAPEX → LT — Cheaper steel helps EPC margins/volumes.
