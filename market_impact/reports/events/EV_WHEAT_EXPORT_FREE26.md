# Government lifts wheat and wheat-product export ban (24 Aug 2026) (`EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_FREE26`)
*agri_rural · status occurred · date 2026-08-24 · P(3m) 1.0 · P(12m) 1.0 · confidence high*

DGFT notification (24 Aug): wheat (incl. durum) export policy revised from 'prohibited' to 'free' with immediate effect; wheat flour, maida, semolina also freed. Follows limited permission in Feb 2026. Rationale: farmers' interests amid depressed local prices after record 2025-26 crop (120.6 MT per Bloomberg) and record projected stocks (USDA); Black Sea/war disruption lifted global prices. Bangladesh sought Indian wheat. Since then monsoon failure and rabi sowing lag (wheat 0.19 vs 3.34 lakh ha on 30 Oct) raise the risk of reversal.

**Already moved:** CBOT wheat proxy: -14.7% 1m, -1.3% since 1 Jun (factor_state); Food stocks (flour millers) rallied on the day per Fortune India.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `F_WHEAT` Wheat price: 0.2 SSU (range -0.3…0.6) Adds Indian supply to global market (bearish) but India's price is not export-competitive; net small.
- `F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN` India food inflation: 0.1 SSU (range 0.0…0.3) Domestic wheat/flour price upside if exports take off.

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_FREE26 → F_WHEAT → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN — Export access supports domestic wheat prices; combined with weak rabi sowing raises 2027 flour inflation risk.
- order 3: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_FREE26 → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN → F_CPI_IN — Cereals (wheat) weight about 3.5% in CPI.
- order 4: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_FREE26 → F_WHEAT → F_PACKAGING — Not material.
