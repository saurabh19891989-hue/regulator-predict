# China enforces steel output cuts / export licensing discipline ('anti-involution') (`EV_CHINA_STEEL_OUTPUT_CURB_2027`)
*metals_china · status anticipated · date 2027-01-31 · P(3m) 0.3 · P(12m) 0.5 · confidence low*

Fastmarkets (17 Sep) reported an anti-price-war policy and industry self-discipline initiative to accelerate supply-side adjustment; CISA (4 Aug) urged mills to comply with export regulations (export licensing). Chinese crude output was falling in Aug (Yahoo 16 Aug) while September steel exports were still 10.49 Mt (+3.3% m/m). A formal 2027 output-control order (typically announced around Dec CEWC / Q1 NPC) would lift global HRC and lower iron ore. Probability of an explicit, enforced cut: ~0.3 by 3m, 0.5 by 12m (judgement).
