# Government permits commercial sugar exports in 2026-27 (`EV_SUGAR_EXPORT_QUOTA_2627`)
*agri_rural · status anticipated · date 2027-03-31 · P(3m) 0.03 · P(12m) 0.07 · confidence low*

With 2025-26 output cut to 306 lakh t, opening stock ~33-34 lakh t, first duty-free imports in a decade, zero ethanol diversion adding ~30 lakh t, but no firm 2026-27 production estimate yet (ISMA awaiting Sept-Oct rain; govt tracking monthly). Export permission would require comfortable stocks and prices near MSP; only the EU TRQ (5,841 t) and US raw sugar quota (extra 542 t) apply.
