# Centre announces drought relief / rural support package (extra VB-G RAM G days, input subsidy, PM-Kisan top-up) (`EV_RURAL_RELIEF_PACKAGE`)
*agri_rural · status anticipated · date 2026-12-31 · P(3m) 0.35 · P(12m) 0.55 · confidence low*

Drought declared in parts of Maharashtra and Karnataka; 282 districts rain-deficient; VB-G RAM G person-days fell ~50% and the minister reports 'no major crisis' (Chouhan, 29 Sep). State governments are announcing own relief (e.g. Chhattisgarh and UP pre-Diwali paddy support; Congress demanding Rs 3,358/q paddy). A central package is more likely after state memoranda and the NDMA/inter-ministerial team reports (Dec-Feb) and is the usual route in drought years; fiscal space tight. Probability 35% by end-Feb, 55% by Nov 2027.

- `F_GSEC_10Y_IN` India 10-year G-sec yield: 0.1 SSU (range 0.0…0.3) Small fiscal impact.
