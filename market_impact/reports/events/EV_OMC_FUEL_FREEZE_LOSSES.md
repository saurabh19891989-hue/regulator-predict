# Retail petrol/diesel price freeze with crude >$100 squeezes OMC marketing margins; Q2 FY27 results split refiners (`EV_OMC_FUEL_FREEZE_LOSSES`)
*sector_policy · status occurred · date 2026-09-01 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence low*

Retail petrol/diesel prices unchanged while crude surged: ICRA/Express/TOI estimated OMC losses of ~Rs530 crore/day (23-24 Sep) and up to Rs30/litre on some sales (TOI 16 Oct); petrol margins briefly turned positive in early Oct (ET 7 Oct) then reversed with Brent $100-110. Private retailers Jio-bp and Nayara raised prices (Jio-bp +Rs5 petrol/+Rs3 diesel, 11 Oct) and capped diesel sales (ET 28 Sep). Q2 FY27 PAT headlines: IOC Rs21,630 cr (swing from Q1 loss; record, helped by LPG compensation and refining margins), HPCL Rs3,625 cr (-6% y/y; another headline says Rs3,483 cr), BPCL Rs4,993 cr (-22.5%, 'fuel price freeze'). Oil PSUs total Rs30,106 cr = 39% of FY26 profit in one quarter (FE 1 Nov). IOC/PSU GRM $/bbl and per-litre marketing margins NOT verified from filings; indicative numbers above are headline-level.

**Already moved:** BPCL target cut to Rs385 (Moneycontrol 27 Oct); BPCL Q2 PAT -22.5% y/y; IOC +184% y/y (The Hindu 30 Oct).

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `NEW_OMC_MARKETING_MARGIN` OMC petrol+diesel marketing margin: -3.0 SSU (range -5.0…-1.5) change vs normal ~Rs3-4/litre combined petrol-diesel margin; SSU +Rs1/litre. Headline losses Rs0-30/litre depending on date; unverified

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_OMC_FUEL_FREEZE_LOSSES → NEW_OMC_MARKETING_MARGIN → BPCL — marketing share of EBITDA is highest for BPCL/HPCL
- order 3: EV_OMC_FUEL_FREEZE_LOSSES → NEW_OMC_MARKETING_MARGIN → F_GOVT_CAPEX — weaker OMC cash flows risk delaying downstream capex and dividends to government
- order 4: EV_OMC_FUEL_FREEZE_LOSSES → NEW_OMC_MARKETING_MARGIN → F_CPI_IN → F_REPO_IN — freeze holds CPI down but defers pass-through; later hike is a CPI/yield risk
