# Cabinet approves higher rabi-season NBS subsidy rates for P&K fertilisers (and DAP special package) (`EV_NBS_RABI_HIKE`)
*agri_rural · status anticipated · date 2026-11-30 · P(3m) 0.85 · P(12m) 0.95 · confidence medium*

As of 25 Oct the NBS rates for rabi (Oct 2026-Mar 2027) were still pending, which kept global DAP prices high and left supply uncertain (RuralVoice). Sulphur/ammonia/phosphoric acid costs high (DAP >US$900/t). Rabi 2026-27 requirement ~38 MT of urea/DAP/NPK. Historically the Cabinet approves NBS rates for rabi in Sep-Nov (model knowledge). Probability approval with higher per-kg subsidy: 85% by end-Feb; 15% of a late/limited or ad hoc special-package route.

- `P_FERT_SUBSIDY` Fertiliser subsidy generosity: 1.0 SSU (range 0.5…1.5) Conditional: higher NBS rates to hold farm-gate MRP flat.
- `F_FERTILISER_INPUTS` Ammonia / urea / phosphate input prices: -0.2 SSU (range -0.5…0.0) Clearer demand signals; imports contracted.
