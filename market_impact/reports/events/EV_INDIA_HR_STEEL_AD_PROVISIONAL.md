# DGTR recommends/imposes anti-dumping duty on hot-rolled flat steel from China, Japan, Russia (`EV_INDIA_HR_STEEL_AD_PROVISIONAL`)
*metals_china · status anticipated · date 2027-03-31 · P(3m) 0.2 · P(12m) 0.55 · confidence low*

DGTR initiated the HR flat-steel AD probe on 26 Jun 2026 (Livemint; GMK 29 Jun); statutory timeline is 12 months (18 max) so final findings fall ~Jun 2027, provisional recommendations possible earlier. Finance-ministry notification is the bottleneck (ET 5 Aug: duties crawl). Counter-signal: Reuters (6 Oct) reports the steel ministry is unlikely to seek new curbs despite an import surge, and H1 FY27 imports rose ~24% (Policy Edge 23 Oct). Safeguard duty (12% year 1, falling 11.5%/11%, model knowledge; imposed 31 Dec 2025 for 3 years per BS/Mint) already protects flat steel. Probability that a notified AD/provisional duty exists by 12m: ~0.45-0.55; by 3m ~0.2. The 30 Oct ET Infra headline 'Government proposes 12% safeguard duty' could not be read - treat as unresolved (may refer to a new product scope).
