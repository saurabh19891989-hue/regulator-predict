# Waiver lapses and US imposes secondary tariff (up to 100%) or sanctions on Indian refiners buying Russian oil (`EV_RUSSIA_TARIFF_ENFORCED`)
*geopolitics_trade · status risk_scenario · date 2026-12-17 · P(3m) 0.1 · P(12m) 0.15 · confidence low*
