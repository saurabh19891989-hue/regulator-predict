# Sugar minimum selling price raised above Rs 31/kg (or panel recommends) (`EV_SUGAR_MSP_HIKE`)
*agri_rural · status anticipated · date 2027-01-31 · P(3m) 0.2 · P(12m) 0.4 · confidence low*

Industry (ISMA) seeks timely alignment of sugar MSP (unchanged at Rs 31/kg since Feb 2019, model knowledge) with FRP (+Rs 10/q for 2026-27) and ethanol prices; Centre set up a panel to review sugar prices and farmers' payments; Karnataka cane farmers are protesting for higher cane price and sugar MSP. Retail sugar at Rs 58/kg is far above MSP, so the government has little reason to raise MSP while prices are high; a hike becomes likelier if prices fall as cane crushing (107 mills started) gets going and ethanol diversion is zero. Probability 20% by end-Jan 2027, 40% by Nov 2027.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `P_SUGAR_ETHANOL` Sugar export / ethanol blending policy: 0.8 SSU (range 0.3…1.2) Conditional: higher MSP favours millers.
- `F_SUGAR` Sugar price: 0.3 SSU (range 0.0…0.6) Raises domestic floor prices.

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_SUGAR_MSP_HIKE → P_SUGAR_ETHANOL → F_SUGAR — Floor price support.
- order 3: EV_SUGAR_MSP_HIKE → F_SUGAR → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN — Small CPI effect.
- order 4: EV_SUGAR_MSP_HIKE → P_SUGAR_ETHANOL → F_RURAL_DEMAND — Cane-belt incomes (UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka).
