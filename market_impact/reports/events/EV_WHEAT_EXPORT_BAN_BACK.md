# Government re-imposes wheat/flour export curbs before the 2027 harvest (`EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_BAN_BACK`)
*agri_rural · status risk_scenario · date 2027-02-15 · P(3m) 0.12 · P(12m) 0.22 · confidence low*

Export ban was lifted only on 24 Aug 2026; weak sowing and rising atta prices could prompt a policy reversal (India's record is to reverse quickly: May 2022 ban). Central Pool stocks are 2.2x norm, which argues against curbs. Probability 12% by early Feb, 22% by end-May 2027.

## Entry shocks (conditional on the event)
- `F_WHEAT` Wheat price: -0.3 SSU (range -0.8…0.0) Global wheat up on Indian absence is already small; domestic prices fall (domestic price channel in F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN).
- `F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN` India food inflation: -0.2 SSU (range -0.4…0.0) Lower domestic cereals inflation.

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_BAN_BACK → F_FOOD_INFLATION_IN → F_CPI_IN — Curb caps flour prices.
- order 3: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_BAN_BACK → F_CPI_IN → F_REPO_IN — Slightly reduces the hiking bias.
- order 4: EV_WHEAT_EXPORT_BAN_BACK → F_REPO_IN → F_GSEC_10Y_IN — Lower yields.
