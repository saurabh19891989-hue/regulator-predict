# India Q1 FY27 GDP grows 7.8% (31 Aug 2026), beating ~7.3% consensus (`EV_GDP_Q1FY27_7_8`)
*india_macro · status occurred · date 2026-08-31 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence high*

April-June 2026 GDP +7.8% y/y per Government/MoSPI, despite the US-Iran war oil shock; driven by consumption and investment (central capex +18% Apr-Aug). Pre-release consensus ~7.3% (Fibre2Fashion report 18 Aug). RBI FY27 forecast raised to 7.1%; IMF 7.0% (13 Oct); Finance Ministry estimates Q2 FY27 at ~7.35% (29 Oct). Manufacturing PMI 55.1 (Sep, 7-month high), 54.7 (Oct); gross GST Rs 2.31 lakh crore in Oct (+22.9% y/y, partly import-driven/low base).

**Already moved:** Strong growth enabled RBI to prioritise inflation (hike). Market effect not isolated (Nifty fell ~6% in September on oil, FPI selling).
