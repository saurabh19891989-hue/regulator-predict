# Domestic PNG incentive scheme: extra 200 SCM cheaper APM gas to CGD firms from 1 Sep 2026 (`EV_PNG_APM_INCENTIVE`)
*sector_policy · status occurred · date 2026-09-01 · P(3m) 1 · P(12m) 1 · confidence low*

Government (PIB 13 Sep; Hindu/BS 18 Aug) allocated additional 200 SCM (units of gas) of cheaper domestic gas to CGD companies to accelerate household PNG connections, effective 1 Sep. Raises APM allocation share, supporting CGD margins and lowering blended gas cost; reduces gas available elsewhere. Quantum in mmscmd, APM price level and allocation % of CGD demand not verified (APM price follows formula linked to imported crude basket, hence rises with Brent).

**Already moved:** Not separable; CGD names not in Nifty 200 except ATGL, GAIL.

- `F_GAS_GLOBAL` Global natural gas / LNG price: -0.2 SSU (range -0.4…0.0) marginal blended gas cost relief for CGD (not market price)

## Causal chains (agent)
- order 2: EV_PNG_APM_INCENTIVE → F_GAS_GLOBAL → ATGL — lower blended gas cost lifts CGD margin
- order 3: EV_PNG_APM_INCENTIVE → F_GAS_GLOBAL → F_CPI_IN — PNG vs LPG substitution lowers LPG subsidy burden
- order 4: EV_PNG_APM_INCENTIVE → F_GAS_GLOBAL → F_URBAN_DEMAND → F_CPI_IN — household fuel bill relief small
