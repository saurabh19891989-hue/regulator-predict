# PAGEIND — Page Industries Ltd. (Midcap 100) — profile as of 2026-11-05

## Business
Exclusive licensee of Jockey International in India, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal and the Middle East (UAE, Oman, Qatar, Maldives, Bhutan; Saudi/Bahrain/Kuwait added in FY25), and of Speedo in India. Revenue Rs5,247 cr in FY26 (TTM Rs5,351 cr), EBITDA margin 21-22%, PAT Rs764 cr, ROE ~54%, dividend payout 80-138%. Q1 FY27: sales Rs1,420 cr (+8% y/y), OPM 20%, PAT Rs193 cr (-4%). Promoters 42.89%, FIIs 18.8% (24.0% in Jun-2025), DIIs 32.9%. Source: screener.in (fetched directly 2026-11-05; the pack had no dossier) and Moneycontrol/NDTV Profit headlines.

## What moves the stock (ranked)
1. **Urban and rural demand (+3.5 / +1.8 per +5pp)**: volume growth is the driver of operating leverage; management's FY27 volume target (~11%) was not matched by Q1 revenue +8%.
2. **Cotton and input costs (-1.2 per +10% cotton; wages -0.4; Brent -0.4)**: margin fell to 20% from 22-23%.
3. **Competition (-4)** from mass brands and D2C.
4. **Valuation/discount rate (-2.0 per +25bp) and FII flows (+2.0)**: 51x P/E; the FII holding fell 5pp in 15 months.
5. **Policy**: GST (+1.0), labour codes (-0.8).

## Transmission examples
- Middle East escalation -> Brent up -> polyester/elastane, packaging and freight up (1st: -0.4) -> INR weaker raises royalty and imported trims (2nd: -0.5) -> food/fuel inflation up (3rd) -> household discretionary spending slows and RBI holds rates (4th: urban demand -3.5 per -5pp) -> volumes miss the 11% target.
- Cotton +10% (already +19.7% over 12m) -> yarn +6% -> gross margin down ~1.4pp unless MRP raised -> Page raises prices 3-4% within 2-3 quarters -> volume elasticity -0.5 trims demand -> margins stabilise at 21% with lower volume.
- FII selling in quality consumer names -> de-rating despite flat fundamentals (stock -10.9% 12m, 5y CAGR -2%).
- GST or income-tax relief -> urban demand up -> premium innerwear volume recovers -> operating leverage on fixed-cost base.
