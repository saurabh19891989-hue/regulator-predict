How the numbers are built

Events feed macro, commodity, policy, geopolitical and theme drivers, which feed companies. Each link is a weighted elasticity in standard shock units, written by research agents from fundamentals and checked against price co-movement. Effects travel up to four links, damped by order, with controls so the same channel is not counted twice.

  • The expected return is half of the probability-weighted forward event exposure plus half of company-specific catalysts, net of beta, centred on the Nifty 200.
  • For market-traded macro drivers (rates, currencies, commodities, equity indices, volatility, flows, growth, inflation), the market's forward path is taken as given. Their scenarios widen the risk bands but add no mean drift.
  • What already moved since June is shown as a diagnostic only. It failed the in-sample check, so it does not enter the headline.
  • Eight independent reviewers checked event facts, unit and sign errors, double counting and every top, bottom and Nifty Next 50 stock. Their fixes are applied as logged overrides.
  • Rankings are frozen in a timestamped, hashed ledger and will be scored at three and twelve months against realised returns and a momentum baseline.

Treat the rankings as an exposure map with modest tilts, not a trading signal.
