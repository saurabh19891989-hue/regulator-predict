## About
Incorporated in 2010, Eternal Limited (previously Zomato Limited) is one of the leading online Food Service platforms in terms of the value of food sold. Its offerings include food delivery, dining-out services, Loyalty programs, and others. As of December 31, 2020, Eternal has established a strong footprint across 23 countries with 131,233 active food delivery restaurants, 161,637 active delivery partners, and an average monthly food order of 10.7 million customers.
